# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two primary audiences, both evaluating José Guilherme as a professional and reaching out through the contact channels:

- **Recruiters and hiring managers** at tech companies, scanning for front-end / software developer candidates; success = quick read of experience and skills, then LinkedIn/email contact.
- **Freelance clients**, evaluating craft on real projects; success = enough confidence to start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of José Guilherme (joseguilherme.vercel.app): presents who he is, his 8 years of experience, his skills (strong front-end with a growing back-end), and two real client projects so visitors can judge competence and contact him via email, LinkedIn, or GitHub. Success means the visitor assesses capability and initiates contact.

## Positioning

A software developer with a wide, production-ready front-end stack (Vue/Nuxt, React/Next, TypeScript) who is actively deepening back-end skills (Node.js, SQL, Docker, authentication) and claims performance, maintainability, and good practices as the work's distinguishing qualities — verified by real client projects shipped to production.

## Operating Context

- Single-page site, pt-BR copy; sections: nav, hero, projects, about, footer.
- Contact paths: mailto (jgtomaine@hotmail.com), GitHub, LinkedIn; located in Bauru/SP, Brasil.
- Deployed on Vercel; content centralized in lib/constants.ts.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Frontend-only (no backend, no CMS); content driven by lib/constants.ts (PERSONAL_INFO, PROJECTS, SKILLS_CATEGORIES).
- Skip-to-content link, focus management, smooth scroll honoring prefers-reduced-motion.
- Content (projects, photo, copy) may evolve over time; must stay cheap to update.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: José Guilherme Paro Monteiro Tomaine; title: "Desenvolvedor de Software".
- Site language: pt-BR. No visual identity commitments were made.

## Evidence on Hand

- Profile photo: public/images/profile.png.
- Two real projects with live links and repositories: Naturella and Studio Amanda Borges (screenshots in public/images/).
- Real contact channels (email, GitHub, LinkedIn). No testimonials or case studies — future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Recruiters and clients must grasp competence and experience within the first viewport.
2. Every factual claim (years, skills, projects) stays true to the record.
3. Content stays centralized and cheap to update as the career evolves.
4. Accessibility (WCAG 2.x AA) is a requirement of the site, not an afterthought.
5. Contact paths are immediate and always visible.
